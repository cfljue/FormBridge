# Mint Transit

Mint Transit treats FormBridge as a quiet piece of infrastructure: a small, dependable bridge between repetitive browser contexts. The composition is built around two pale interface-like planes joined by a single flowing green path. The bridge is present as motion and alignment rather than as a literal illustration, keeping the identity useful at both social-preview and store-tile scales.

The palette uses misty mint, warm off-white, and deep evergreen. Mint communicates assistance and progress without borrowing the urgency of security-red or the coldness of enterprise-blue. Deep evergreen provides the visual anchor and makes the name readable, while restrained lime highlights suggest an action completing successfully.

Form is deliberately spacious. Rounded field modules echo browser forms, but they are reduced to calm geometric marks rather than screenshots. Their meticulous spacing, consistent radii, and repeated baselines make the work feel engineered; tiny status dots and connector details reward close inspection without becoming decorative noise.

Typography is direct and confident, using Bricolage Grotesque for the product name and Instrument Sans for the supporting phrase. The hierarchy remains intentionally shallow: one strong name, one short promise, and no feature list. This lets the artwork communicate at thumbnail size and preserves the meticulous visual rhythm of the surrounding forms.

Across every output, craftsmanship comes from alignment rather than ornament. The connector lands exactly on field centers, shadows stay soft and low, and the logo retains clear breathing room. The result should feel local-first, lightweight, and trustworthy—a polished utility that moves information only when its user asks it to.
